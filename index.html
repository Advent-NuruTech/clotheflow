Core Modules

EVERYTHING A TAILOR NEEDS

One system that replaces notebooks, spreadsheets, and confusion — built specifically for tailoring businesses across Africa.

👤
Customer Records

Store full profiles with photos, body measurements, style preferences, and complete history. Find any customer instantly by name, phone, or order number.

📏
Accurate Measurements

Record all body measurements — waist, chest, shoulder, arm length, and more. Track changes over time and reuse old sizes with one click.

🧵
Material Inventory

Track fabrics by name, type, color, quantity, and supplier. Smart auto-calculation tells you exactly how many garments you can make from remaining stock.

📋
Order Management

Follow every order from measurement to delivery through 7 clear stages. Visual dashboard shows green, yellow, and red status at a glance.

🔔
Smart Notifications

Automatic SMS and WhatsApp alerts keep customers informed — order received, work in progress, ready for pickup, and payment reminders.

📊
Business Reports

Daily and monthly analytics: top materials, best customers, most loved colors, tailor performance, income, and expenses — all in one place.

💰
Financial Tracking

Track deposits, full payments, balances, and expenses. Generate PDF receipts with shop logo and QR codes. M-Pesa integration coming soon.

👷
Staff Management

Manage tailor profiles, track productivity, assign tasks, and measure performance. Know who completed what, how fast, and with what quality.

📱
Customer Portal

Give customers a portal to track their order, view designs, reorder previous clothes, download receipts, and message the shop directly.

The Process

ORDER WORKFLOW

Every order moves through clear stages — so nothing is forgotten and every customer gets their clothes on time.

1
Measurement
Customer details and body measurements recorded and saved to their profile
2
Material
Fabric selected from inventory. Stock automatically updated
3
Cutting
Order assigned to tailor. Cutting stage begins with timer tracking
4
Sewing
Active sewing phase. Customer receives a progress update notification
5
Ironing
Finishing touches. Quality check completed before dispatch
6
Ready
WhatsApp/SMS sent automatically. Invoice generated with pickup time
7
Delivered
Order closed. Receipt issued. Customer history updated for next visit
